# ThystTV 1.3.0

ThystTV 1.3 improves player controls, large-screen viewing, live discovery and local
Stats, with a more resilient updater and focused chat and playback fixes.

## Player and gestures

- Quality, Speed, Stream volume and More use consistent player overlays with clearer
  selection states, fixed headers and reachable options on small and wide windows.
- The Quality gear stays in place through rotation; the menu keeps readable quality
  and codec labels, including audio-only and chat-only transitions.
- Landscape display modes are now Fit, Fill and Stretch. Pinch between Fit and Fill
  with visual feedback, and use the gesture guide to discover playback controls.
- Brightness, device volume and stream volume have clearer gesture feedback. The
  mini-player and controls adapt more consistently to tablets and resized windows.
- Closed players release their landscape system-bar listener, fixing a retained
  player reference after rotation or switching.
- Reopening a completed saved VoD or downloaded video starts at the beginning.
  Unfinished videos keep their saved position; explicit timestamps remain respected.
- Unhandled headset previous/next buttons seek once on the legacy ExoPlayer path.

## Discovery, chat and Stats

- Channel search and profiles show live status and provide direct access to live streams.
- VoD chat replay stays responsive at high playback speeds, including downloaded chat.
- Followed-channel paging retains fetched channels beyond the first page.
- Native Twitch emotes appear in autocomplete even when third-party emotes are disabled;
  refreshing suggestions preserves third-party emotes and chatter completions.
- Channel social links show their destination host. Compact Markdown headings render
  correctly in channel panels and team descriptions.
- Stats has more compact summary cards, readable range controls and charts that handle
  small totals, wide layouts and rapid range changes more smoothly.

## Updates and appearance

- The update prompt shows version details, rendered release notes and actions that
  remain reachable with large text and during window resizing.
- Downloads and installer results survive screen pauses and recreation while the app
  process remains alive. Updater entry points share one operation, with cancel, retry
  and browser recovery.
- Install permission is requested when needed, and installation callbacks use a private,
  session-specific receiver. The launcher no longer forwards nested installer intents.
- Refreshed the gem launcher icon, including adaptive, themed and legacy variants.
- Restored accented and non-Latin text in 13 locales after an encoding regression.

## Upgrading

The landscape display setting migrates automatically on first use: the old Fit option
stays Fit; the old Fill/Zoom variants become Fill; Stretch stays Stretch. The original
preference remains available as migration input. No database schema migration is added.

Install the official signed APK over the existing app to retain its account, settings,
Stats, bookmarks and downloads. A debug build is a separate app and does not test an
official upgrade. For unfinished downloads originally started on 1.2.0, the existing
1.2.1 guidance still applies: delete and restart those downloads.

## Known limitations

Updater downloads do not resume after process death. If Android closes the app process,
start a fresh update attempt. Cronet and HttpEngine may report progress only when the
download finishes. New updater captions use English fallback where translations are
unavailable. This release does not add full Android TV remote-navigation support.

See the [APK verification guide](https://github.com/tzii/ThystTV/blob/master/docs/APK_VERIFICATION.md)
for checksum and official signing-certificate verification.
